title: Family code word
description: "A five-minute activity for choosing a family code word that settles any emergency call in seconds, even one with a cloned voice."
---

**Time:** 5 minutes in the session, or as homework. **Goal:** each person leaves with a plan to choose a code word with their family, in person, this week.

## Why it works

Emergency scams depend on panic and a believable voice. A short word only your family knows ends the call in seconds: "What's our word?" A scammer won't know it, however real the voice sounds.

## Choosing a good word

- **Easy to remember under stress.** A silly phrase, an old pet's name with a twist, a family joke.
- **Not findable online.** Nothing from social media, such as a current pet's name, a street, or a school.
- **Shared in person**, or on a phone call you started yourself. Never by text, email, or social media.
- **Everyone knows it**, including grandparents and kids old enough to answer the phone.

## The rule that goes with it

If someone calls in an emergency and asks for money, help, or secrecy, ask for the word. If they can't give it, hang up and call the person directly on the number you already have.

## In the session

Ask people to write down, on the back of their [takeaway](../handouts/one-page-takeaway.md), who they'll set a word with and when. Don't ask anyone to say a word out loud.
