description: "Hands-on activities for a scam awareness session: sorting real and fake messages, practicing the pause, turning on two-step sign-in, and choosing a family code word."
---

| Exercise | Time | What it builds |
|---|---|---|
| [Spot the scam](spot-the-scam.md) | 10 min | Recognizing the four pressures in real messages |
| [Role-plays](role-plays.md) | 5 to 10 min | The confidence to hang up and call back |
| [Two-step sign-in lab](two-step-lab.md) | 10 to 15 min | Two-step sign-in on email, done in the room |
| [Family code word](family-code-word.md) | 5 min, or homework | A plan for emergency calls |

Every session should include the lab. It's the one change that protects people even when they miss a scam.
