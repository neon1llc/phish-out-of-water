---
title: Job and task scams
description: "Job offers that pay for simple online tasks, ask you to deposit a check and send some back, or charge fees for training or equipment."
---

## What it looks like

- A text: "We saw your resume. Earn $300 a day liking videos or rating products from home."
- Simple tasks pay at first, then you're asked to deposit your own money (often crypto) to "unlock" bigger tasks.
- A job that sends you a check to buy equipment from "our vendor," and asks you to send back the rest. The check bounces days later.
- An interview held only by chat, followed by a request for your Social Security number and bank details before any contract.

## The tell

- You didn't apply, or the pay is far too good for the work.
- **You pay them**: for training, equipment, or to unlock earnings.
- A check to deposit and forward, or a request to receive and reship packages.

## What to do

Look up the company yourself and contact it through its official website. Real employers don't ask you to pay to work, or to send money back from a check. Don't share your Social Security number until you've confirmed the job and signed an offer.

## If it already happened

Stop sending money. Tell your bank about any check you deposited. If you shared your Social Security number, go to **IdentityTheft.gov** and freeze your credit. Report it at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**.
