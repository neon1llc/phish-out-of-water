---
title: Government impostors
description: "Callers pretending to be the IRS, Social Security, Medicare, the courts, or the police, demanding payment or personal details."
---

## What it looks like

"This is the Social Security Administration. Your number has been suspended due to suspicious activity." "You missed jury duty and there's a warrant for your arrest." "You owe back taxes and will be arrested today unless you pay." Some scammers give fake badge numbers or send official-looking letters by email.

## The tell

- Threats of arrest, deportation, or losing benefits, **today**.
- Payment by gift card, wire, crypto, or cash handed to a courier.
- "Don't hang up" or "don't tell anyone."

Government agencies contact people mainly by mail, don't demand immediate payment by phone, and never take gift cards.

## What to do

Hang up. Look up the agency's number on its official website (they end in `.gov`) and call to ask. Never confirm your Social Security number or bank details to an incoming caller.

## If it already happened

Call your bank. If you shared your Social Security number, go to **IdentityTheft.gov** and place a credit freeze with all three bureaus. Report the call at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**.
