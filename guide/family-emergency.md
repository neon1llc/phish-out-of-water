title: Grandparent and voice clone emergencies
description: "A call from a 'grandchild' or other loved one in an accident or in jail, needing money now and asking you not to tell anyone."
---

## What it looks like

A panicked voice: "Grandma, it's me. I'm in trouble." An accident, an arrest, a hospital abroad. Then a "lawyer" or "officer" takes over and explains how to send bail money, sometimes by a courier who comes to your door.

Scammers can now copy a voice from a few seconds of video posted online, so it may really sound like your grandchild.

## The tell

- **Secrecy:** "Don't tell Mom and Dad."
- **Hurry:** "I only get one phone call."
- Payment in cash, gift cards, wire, or crypto, or a courier collecting money.

## What to do

Hang up and call your grandchild on the number you already have, or call their parents. Ask for the [family code word](../exercises/family-code-word.md) if you've set one. Real police and courts don't collect bail by courier or gift card.

## If it already happened

Call your bank immediately. If you gave cash to a courier, call local police right away; couriers are sometimes caught on a second visit. Report it at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**, and if you're 60 or older, call the National Elder Fraud Hotline at **1-833-FRAUD-11** (1-833-372-8311).
