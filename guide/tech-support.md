title: Tech support pop-ups and calls
description: "A screen warning that your computer is infected, or a call from 'Microsoft' or 'Apple,' leading to remote access and payment."
---

## What it looks like

A loud warning fills your browser: "Your computer is infected! Do not close this window. Call Microsoft Support at [number]." Sometimes it beeps or a voice speaks. Or someone calls saying they're from a tech company and have detected a problem on your computer.

The "technician" asks you to install a remote access app, shows you scary-looking screens, then asks for payment, often in gift cards or by "refunding" an amount they claim they overpaid.

## The tell

- Real tech companies don't put phone numbers in warning pop-ups, and don't call you out of the blue.
- They want **remote access** to your computer.
- They want payment by gift card, wire, crypto, or into your bank account through a "refund."

## What to do

Don't call the number. Close the browser. If it won't close, force it to quit (Ctrl+Alt+Delete and Task Manager on Windows, or Command+Option+Escape on a Mac) or restart the computer. If someone calls, hang up.

## If it already happened

Disconnect the computer from the internet. Uninstall the remote access app, or have someone you trust check the computer. Change passwords from a different device, starting with email and banking. Call your bank if you paid or let them see your bank account.
