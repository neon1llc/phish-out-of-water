---
title: Online marketplace and overpayment scams
description: "Fake buyers and sellers on marketplace apps: overpayments, fake payment emails, verification codes, and items that never arrive."
---

## What it looks like

**As a seller:** a buyer "accidentally" pays too much and asks you to refund the difference, then the original payment bounces. Or they send an email that looks like it's from a payment service saying the money is waiting. Or they ask you to read a code "to prove you're real," which is really setting up an account in your name.

**As a buyer:** a great deal, a seller who wants payment by a payment app's "friends and family" option or a gift card, and an item that never comes. Rental listings work the same way: a deposit for an apartment that isn't theirs.

## The tell

- Overpayments, refunds, or "pay the shipping and I'll reimburse you."
- Moving off the platform to text or email.
- Payment methods with no buyer protection.
- Requests for a verification code.

## What to do

Keep the conversation and payment inside the platform. Meet in a public place, ideally one the police suggest for exchanges, and check items before paying. Use payment methods with buyer protection. For rentals, see the place in person and confirm who owns it.

## If it already happened

Report the user to the platform. Contact your payment app or bank right away. Report it at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**.
