Each page follows the same shape: **what it looks like**, **the tell**, **what to do**, and **if it already happened**. The four pressures (hurry, fear, secrecy, and an odd way to pay) run through all of them.

| Scam | Arrives by | Most often aimed at |
|---|---|---|
| [Phishing emails](phishing-email.md) | Email | Everyone |
| [Package, toll, and account texts](scam-texts.md) | Text | Everyone |
| [Fake fraud departments and code theft](code-theft.md) | Phone | Everyone |
| [Tech support pop-ups and calls](tech-support.md) | Browser, phone | Older adults |
| [Government impostors](government-impostor.md) | Phone | Everyone |
| [Grandparent and voice clone emergencies](family-emergency.md) | Phone | Grandparents, parents |
| [Invoice and bank change fraud](payment-change.md) | Email | Offices, bookkeepers |
| [Boss and gift card requests](boss-gift-cards.md) | Email, text | Workplace teams |
| [Romance and investment scams](romance-investment.md) | Social media, dating apps, wrong-number texts | Adults of every age |
| [Online marketplace and overpayment scams](marketplace.md) | Marketplace apps | Buyers and sellers |
| [Job and task scams](job-scams.md) | Text, social media, job sites | Students, job seekers |

When something already went wrong, the [who-to-call card](../handouts/who-to-call.md) has the first steps, and Ready, Secure, Go has full [playbooks](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/tree/main/content/playbooks).
