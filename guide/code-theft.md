---
title: Fake fraud departments and code theft
description: "A caller claiming to be your bank's fraud team, or a tech company, who asks you to read back a sign-in or verification code."
---

## What it looks like

"This is the fraud department at your bank. We've blocked a suspicious charge. To verify you, I'm sending a code to your phone. Please read it back." The caller ID may even show the bank's real name or number, because caller ID is easy to fake.

Variations: "This is Apple support, someone's trying to get into your iCloud," or "This is your mobile carrier, we need to confirm a SIM change."

## The tell

**They ask for a code you received.** That code is the second key to your account. The caller already has your password, or is trying to reset it, and the code is the last thing they need. A real fraud team never needs it.

## What to do

Hang up. Call the number on the back of your card, or open the company's official app. If there's really a problem, they'll tell you. Never read a code to anyone, ever.

## If it already happened

Call the bank or company right away using a number you looked up. Change the account's password and check its recovery email and phone haven't been changed. If it was your phone carrier, ask them to add a PIN or port-out lock to your account.
