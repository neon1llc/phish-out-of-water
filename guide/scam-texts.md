---
title: Package, toll, and account texts
description: "Text messages about a held package, an unpaid toll, or a problem with an account, with a link to fix it."
---

## What it looks like

- "Your package is on hold. Update your address: [link]"
- "Unpaid toll balance of $4.15. Pay by today to avoid a $50 late fee: [link]"
- "Your account has been suspended. Verify now: [link]"
- "Is this you in this video?" or "Hi, is this Sarah?" from an unknown number.

## The tell

- A link you didn't ask for, to a site you didn't choose.
- A small amount of money. Scammers want your card details, not the $4.
- An odd sender: a long number, an email address, or a number from another country.
- The "wrong number" text that turns into a friendly chat is often the start of a [romance or investment scam](romance-investment.md).

## What to do

Don't tap the link or reply. USPS texts only people who signed up for tracking, and those texts don't contain links. If you're expecting a package, check tracking in the seller's app. For tolls, open your toll account the way you normally do. Forward scam texts to **7726** (it spells SPAM) and delete them.

## If it already happened

If you entered card details, call your card company using the number on the card. If you entered a password, change it and turn on two-step sign-in.
