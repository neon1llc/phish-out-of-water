# Changelog

Phish Out of Water releases use `v0.MINOR.PATCH` until the first complete release, v1.0.0, following neon1's [versioning standard](https://neon1.tech/knowledge/standards/versioning/). Each version below has an annotated tag on the commit it names; the release workflow adds missing tags and never moves one.

## v0.1.1 - 2026-11-12

### Changed
- Every session page, exercise, handout, and field guide entry ends with a Sources section linking the FTC, FBI, USPIS, Justice Department, AARP, NCMEC, and CISA pages behind it.
- Plainer wording throughout, with fewer absolutes and no slogan lines.

### Fixed
- The National Elder Fraud Hotline and AARP helpline entries list their hours, and no longer claim an age limit or membership terms their pages don't state.
- Gift card steps include asking the card company for your money back, as the FTC advises.
- Sextortion advice follows NCMEC: get help before deciding anything about paying, block the account, and keep the messages.
- The USPS card says USPS texts only people who asked for tracking, and those texts contain no links.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-12

### Added
- A one-hour session: run sheet, a Marp slide deck, and a facilitator script.
- Four exercises: spot the scam, role-plays, the two-step sign-in lab, and the family code word.
- Handouts: the one-page takeaway, a who-to-call card, a before-and-after quiz, and a feedback form.
- A field guide to eleven common scams, and notes for adapting the hour to families and seniors, workplace teams, and students.
