# Phish Out of Water: keep this by the phone

## Spot the pressure

Every scam uses at least one of these. **One: slow down. Two or more: stop.**

- **Hurry:** "within the hour," "today only," "before it's too late"
- **Fear:** a locked account, a warrant, a virus, a loved one in trouble
- **Secrecy:** "don't tell the bank," "keep this between us"
- **Odd payment:** gift cards, wire transfers, crypto, payment apps, cash by courier

A real bank, agency, or company **never** asks for gift cards or crypto, and **never** asks you to read back a code.

## Use the pause

1. **Hang up** or close the message.
2. **Look up** the real number yourself: the back of your card, the official app, a bill.
3. **Call back** on that number.

Never use a link, number, or button from the message that asked.

## Lock the front door

- [ ] Two-step sign-in is on for my email (I did it today, or I'll do it by ________).
- [ ] My recovery phone and email are current.
- [ ] A different password for each account, kept in a password manager.
- [ ] Updates on for my phone and computer.

## Know who to tell

- **Family code word:** I'll set one with ____________________ by ________. (Never write the word here.)
- **At work, report suspicious messages to:** ____________________
- **If money is gone:** call your bank's number from the back of your card **right away**, then report it. See the who-to-call card.
